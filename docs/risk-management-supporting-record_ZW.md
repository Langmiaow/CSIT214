# Risk Management Supporting Record

> This file is supporting evidence for the Final Report.
> It does not repeat the full Risk Management section already written in the report.

## 1. Purpose

This record shows how the project risks identified in the Final Report are monitored and reviewed during project execution.

The project team will update this record when:
- a major milestone is reached;
- a new issue is identified;
- a risk becomes more or less serious;
- corrective action is taken; or
- a proposed change may affect project scope, schedule, cost or quality.

## 2. Risk Review Log

|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Requirement uncertainty | Open | Check whether any requirements remain unclear after client/tutor feedback | Update requirements only after confirmed clarification |

| R7 | Cost / effort variance | Open | Compare updated effort with the approved estimate | Escalate if additional effort materially affects the baseline |
| R8 | Access and data handling | Open | Review administrative access and input-validation behaviour | Correct access-control or validation issues before release |

## 3. Risk Escalation Rule

A risk should be escalated when its likelihood or impact increases enough to affect the approved project baseline.

Escalated risks may require:
- corrective action;
- schedule adjustment;
- additional testing;
- scope clarification; or
- a formal change request.
