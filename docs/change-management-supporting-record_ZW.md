## 3. Required Actions After Receiving a Change Request

When the tutor/client provides a formal change request, the team will:

1. record the request and its source;
2. identify the affected requirement or deliverable;
3. assess impact on scope, schedule, cost, resources, risk, design and testing;
4. prepare a recommendation;
5. wait for the authorised decision;
6. update affected project documents if approved;
7. implement and test the approved change; and
8. record verification and closure evidence.
