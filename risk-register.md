# 8. Risk Management

## 8.1 Risk Management Approach

Risk management is used to identify, assess and control uncertainties that may affect the successful delivery of the Community Facility and Maintenance Management System. The main purpose is to reduce the likelihood that project problems will negatively affect the approved scope, schedule, cost or quality of the system.

Each identified risk is assessed based on its likelihood and impact. A priority level is then assigned so that the project team can focus more attention on risks that may have a greater effect on the project. A suitable response strategy is also defined for each risk.

The main risks identified for this project include unclear or changing requirements, scope expansion, development delays, integration problems, resource availability, booking conflicts, cost increases and inappropriate access to system data.

## 8.2 Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Priority | Risk Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Client requirements remain unclear or change after development begins | Medium | High | High | Confirm important requirements early and require significant changes to follow the formal change-control process. |
| R2 | Project scope expands beyond the agreed release | Medium | High | High | Maintain clear project inclusions and exclusions and assess new feature requests against schedule and budget. |
| R3 | Prototype development takes longer than planned | Medium | High | High | Prioritise the core booking workflow, monitor important tasks regularly and reserve time for corrective work. |
| R4 | Integration between booking functions produces technical defects | Medium | High | High | Integrate system functions incrementally and conduct functional and integration testing before final delivery. |
| R5 | Team member availability affects planned project activities | Medium | Medium | Medium | Assign clear task ownership, review progress regularly and redistribute non-critical tasks when required. |
| R6 | Booking conflicts or invalid user input are handled incorrectly | Low-Medium | High | High | Apply clear input validation and perform conflict-detection testing using different booking scenarios. |
| R7 | Project cost or required effort exceeds the planned estimate | Low-Medium | High | Medium-High | Monitor project effort and cost regularly and maintain contingency for unexpected work. |
| R8 | Booking information or administrative functions are accessed incorrectly | Low-Medium | High | Medium-High | Apply authorised access controls, input validation and suitable data-protection practices. |

## 8.3 Risk Monitoring and Control

Project risks will be reviewed regularly during team meetings and at major project milestones. High-priority risks will receive greater attention because they may have a significant effect on project delivery.

Requirement and scope risks will be monitored by reviewing unresolved requirements and newly proposed features. Schedule risks will be monitored by comparing actual project progress with the planned schedule. Technical and integration risks will be monitored through functional and end-to-end testing. Cost risks will be monitored against the planned effort and project budget.

If the likelihood or impact of an existing risk increases, the project team will review the current response strategy and decide whether additional corrective action is required. If the required response affects the approved project scope, schedule, cost or major deliverables, the issue will be managed through the formal change-management process.
